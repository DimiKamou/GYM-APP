## Short description (max 80)

## Full description (max 4000)
PowerHouse is your gym's training log: coaches record every set on the floor, and every athlete sees their own progress on their phone.

FOR COACHES
• One sheet per athlete you can read in five seconds: warnings, the latest note, what was lifted last time and who wrote it.
• One-handed set logging: a big numeric pad, "same as before", supersets in one round, time and load for timed work.
• Works with no signal. What you log in the basement syncs as soon as you are back online.
• Every number carries a date and a name — "80×8 · 12 Aug · Maria" — so a coach covering for a colleague knows exactly where to pick up.
• Per-athlete programmes with an order of days; the next day is offered automatically.
• A shared appointment calendar for the whole team.
• Notes that are never overwritten: a correction is a new note, never an edit of the old one.

FOR ATHLETES
• Sign in with Google, Microsoft, Apple or email, from the link your gym sends you.
• See your workouts, your week streak, your personal records and progress charts per exercise.
• Coaches' internal notes are never shown to athletes.

FOR THE OWNER
• Invite coaches and athletes by link; decide who writes programmes.
• Share progress by link or QR code for a chosen period, revocable at any time.
• Print an athlete's sheet for when a phone dies.

Dark and light themes, Greek and English. Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt).

PowerHouse is for gyms and their athletes. Athlete accounts are created by invitation from the gym.
